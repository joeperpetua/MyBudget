# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 3rd May 2025

MyBudget is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within our Android application, MyBudget.

## No Personal Data Collected
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app functions entirely without requiring your name, email address, location, or any other personal data.

## Local Data Storage
All data you enter into the app (such as incomes and expenses) is stored locally on your device using Android’s SharedPreferences API. None of this information is transmitted to us or to any third parties.

## No Third-Party Services
MyBudget does not use third-party services such as analytics, advertising platforms, or cloud-based storage.

## No User Accounts
You are not required to create an account or sign in to use MyBudget. The app is fully functional without user registration.

## General Audience
This app is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed at children. However, we do not knowingly collect data from any users, including minors.

## Data Security
While data is stored locally, we encourage users to secure their devices with proper lock mechanisms. Since we do not collect or store your data on external servers, data loss is your responsibility in case of device reset or app uninstallation.
